About Reeve's Cancer Support Program

Reeve's Cancer Support Program is dedicated to transforming the journey of children battling cancer and their families. We believe that financial hardship should never stand in the way of a child receiving life-saving pediatric oncology care, surgeries, and compassionate support.

Through our comprehensive network of medical specialists, family counselors, and child life programs, we stand side-by-side with every family, bringing comfort, courage, and clinical excellence.

  • Direct funding for critical pediatric oncology treatments
  • Emotional and psychological counseling for patients & parents
  • Specialized child life therapies and therapeutic comfort items

Our Core Programs

Comprehensive medical, surgical, and emotional support for children facing cancer.

Pediatric Oncology Care

Providing specialized treatments, chemotherapy support, medications, and routine oncological monitoring tailored specifically for developing bodies.

Surgical & Hospital Support

Covering crucial surgical procedures, hospital stays, specialized medical equipment, and pediatric recovery care in top partner hospitals.

Family Financial & Counseling

Alleviating the overwhelming economic burden with family relief grants, lodging assistance, and professional psychological counseling.

Child Life & Recreation

Bringing joy and normalcy through play therapy, art workshops, educational tutors, and uplifting activities during hospital confinement.
